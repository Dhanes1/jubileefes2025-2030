March: First Day of Grade 8!

It is the first day of grade 8 already! Other schools are still in grade 7 while we are 3 months faster than them. Our homeroom teacher and subject teachers are still the same! We just need another 3 months until another holiday for 1 month!

30 March 2026:

The first day of school after the 1 month holiday was fun. At that day (30 March 2026), we are already called 8A (while some teachers forgot) and there was also a Character Development Program again about monsters in our body. At the program, we make a Dream Catcher then after that, we pass papers to tell the positive things about each of our friends and what should they improve.

April: Unit Test and Quiz Month!

We are already back again for unit test and quizzes. It might be boring but it's useful!

2 April - 5 April:

We have a 4-day long holiday! Some people are going to other city and having a staycation. While some are just at home doing nothing.

13 April - 20 April:

In this week we have math quiz, physics, and competitions. We also have EL project to explain Figurative Language by making groups. We also shared lots of candies and foods as a reward. One of the competitions are decorating a tumpeng, making an inspirational video, and also attending the event. Some competitions are even paid. This week is a hard week because we have lots of unit tests and quizzes!

May: Semester Exam Time!

We did all our semester exams at the end of the month, phew! There is 2 subjects each day!

12 May:

At this day, we have field trip together to Taman Mini Indonesia Indah. We went there using 2 busses. When we reached there, we directly go to Museum Transportasi at this museum, the guide told us about the old vehicles like old Bluebird and old Silverbird. We also ride a locomotive train that still works! We also went to Hyundai future inovation place. It was cool and Hyundai is thinking to make a drone-like-helicopter that is a public transportation in about 2030 in Jakarta. Then, we go eat HokBen together outside the museum. After that, we go to Museum Batik. There are alot of rules there like no flash, no talking, no running, and always have 2 lines. Good thing we can still take a photo and video. Although we don't have much time at Museum Batik. It only took us about 30 minutes to go around the whole museum. After that, we go home agoin using the bus. That was a fun and exhausting day for everyone!
